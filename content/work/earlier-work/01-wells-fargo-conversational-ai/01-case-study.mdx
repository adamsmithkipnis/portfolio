From 2018 to 2021, I worked at Wells Fargo, designing product concepts for conversational AI, internal tools for conversation designers, conversation analytics and machine learning. All of these efforts served as the foundation for Wells Fargo's chatbot, Fargo, which launched in 2022.

Some of the challenges faced and overcame were working with emerging technology in a highly regulated environment where usage of off-the-shelf tools was often prohibited for security and compliance reasons. As a result, we designed and built many of the tools that were needed to build the Fargo chatbot.

Try out Fargo here... [https://sites.wf.com/fargo/](https://sites.wf.com/fargo/)

## Case Study

### Business Context

- Bank was interested in leveraging the potential of AI and automation
- Third largest bank in the US
- 71 million customers using mobile banking

### Constraints

- Highly regulated environment
- Trust affected by legacy accounting scandal
- Major focus on risk management, which slowed the pace of innovation

### Design Transformation

- I performed competetive research and created the initial bot persona strategy
- Championed Ethics and Values for AI
- Led the design of voice-first multimodal prototypes for mortgages, with multi-disciplinary team (Google Assistant & Siri)
- Led design of internal conversation design tool
  - Initiated cross-group “show and tell” huddles with conversation designers and PMs across three time zones, to share best practices and get regular feedback on usability and needs.
  - Performed market research on conversational AI design tool interface trends
  - Designed analytics views (both integrated with tool and external tableau dashboard), emoji support, simulator, various features...
- Led the design of transcript annotation for machine learning
  - Reduced annotation time by 50% saving the company time and money
- Created blueprint of the bank’s entire AI ecosystem with recommendations
  - Blueprint and recommendations were used by product management to onboard executives and act on opportunities

### Outcome & Data

- Measurable savings of time and money
- Increase in positive feedback on UX for conversation designers
- Soft launch on Mobile and Web to 71 million customers
- The tools and templates I designed were extensively used
