In addition to being accountable for the audio of the XBox version of ED:R, and doing lots of content creation and implementation, I also did the Cinematic Audio Post-production for a number of Evil Dead Regeneration videos. In this particular video, I tried to imagine the sound of a security guard flying through the air. Keys came to mind. The gun slide was recorded using a metal staple gun in the linoleum tiled hallway downstairs from our offices.
