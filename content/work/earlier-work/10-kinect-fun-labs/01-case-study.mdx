---
title: "Kinect Fun Labs"
---

Kinect Fun Labs was a 3 month effort to create a new set of lightweight experiences to showcase Kinect's capabilities. We took many of the early ideas that we'd had for experiences we wanted to create, and built them into bite-sized experiences. On Kinect Fun Labs, I supervised two contractors and taught them how to implement audio for Kinect using the Wwise Audio Engine and Unreal Game Engine.
